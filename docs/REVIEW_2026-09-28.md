## 5. Where the documents and the code disagree

The ones that would mislead a design:

1. **ARCHITECTURE section 3** says the page table and the packed-mip floor come with a tenant and
   that `hal::Retire` is the only way a tile is released. `PageTable` has no caller outside
   `GaTest.cpp`; packed mips are refused (`Residency.cpp:148`); `Retire()` has no caller.
2. **SPARSE_GA section 18**, "never truncate a page's chain": the page tenants stop at the one-tile
   level on purpose (`Residency.cpp:108-125`). **Sections 16 and 17**, "1024 slices of 16384
   squared": measured for R16F with one mip; in RGBA8 with the chain the same address space holds
   384.
3. **ALGEBRA priors 1** says a bindless `SampleLevel` returns zero in the mesh stage. The mesh
   stage calls `ComposedHeight` and `CsMaskSample`, which sample (`GlobeMesh.hlsl:90`, `:98`), and
   `DxTest.cpp:352-354` says it works.
4. **ALGEBRA frames**, "every water consumer shares the map": the bank's bed uses the exact place
   and the solver and churn the anchor chart. "All CPU sites run doubles": `BathyModel.h:61-67`
   holds floats.
5. **ALGEBRA wavefield and fold** describe 16 components and a smoothstep fold; the code has 32
   and a logistic with a sinc squared (`WaveField.cpp:333-422`; `WaterBank.hlsl:602-604`).
6. **ATLAS section 4**, the rung rule and "filter linear": no realization picks its rung from its
   sources, and the colour fold averages encoded bytes.
7. **`PageSample.hlsli:43-44`**, pages at equal resolution "hold the same pixels": see finding 4.
8. **`Globe.hlsl:773-774`** says the vertex-shaded sea stays the default. Pixel water is.
9. **ARCHITECTURE section 0**, "three kinds of tenant on one lattice": five page tenants on six
   lattices.
10. **`SurfaceFrame.h:24-29`**, the origins are written "nowhere else": true of the two origins;
    `harvest_gis.py:118-119`, `Export.cpp:52` and `PackTiles.cpp:27` write a window of their own,
    and the zoom, the page size and the slices 6 and 7 are literal at some twenty further sites.
11. **`Lattice.h:23-24`** and `SelfTest.cpp:33-34` name a gate, "spacetest block 5 (GroundRes

About 83 GiB of address space in all, 52 of it the wave tenant's. The adapter's address bits are
queried at no site; the only ceiling known is one resource's, from the recorded probe.

**What a slice is.** Fixed at creation: the resource, the stamp, want and slot arrays (14 bytes a
virtual tile), the residency map and its views. No call activates, moves or releases one slice.
No call maps one heap tile at two places. A dirty byte re-sends its whole tenant's residency map,
and the staging area caps that at 256 slices a turn across all tenants.

**How a page is found.** By containment in one Mercator window whose rows ride the surface
constant buffer, at 15 shader sites; the detail page is reachable only from inside the z14
window. There is no list of pages for a world. Through a gate to Haulover the colour, the height
and the mask are the cube's 611 m and nothing finer exists there.

**The anchor chart** has 25 consumers; 14 are wrong 1,900 km away.

**The pixel stage** resolves a page 19 times a pixel at worst and makes 71 to 87 page-tenant reads
for a shore pixel inside the z17 window.

**The water is addressed five ways:** ring metres on the root's tangent plane (the bank), the
anchor chart (the solver, the churn, the wave window), the Mercator page (the bed, the exposure),
the wave chart's cells (the cascades), and latitude and longitude (the tide atlas, the currents).
Findings 12 and 14 of section 1 and most of the water report's list are disagreements between two
of them.

**The CPU mirrors the GPU's structure.** `HeightPage` answers from the z14 page inside the
Merrimack and from the cube's mip 0 elsewhere, so a hull at Haulover stands on a 611 m average
whatever the height stack holds.
